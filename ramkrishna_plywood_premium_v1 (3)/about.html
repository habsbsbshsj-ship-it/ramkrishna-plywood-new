About Ramkrishna Plywood & Laminates

Kolkata's Reliable Partner in Superior Quality Plywood and Interior Supplies

Our Legacy & Commitment

Founded and owned by Bilas Das, Ramkrishna Plywood & Laminates has earned a reputation as a leading wholesaler and dealer of plywood, interior boards, doors, and adhesives in Rajarhat, Kolkata.

Located conveniently at Hatiara, Rajarhat Main Road, Kolkata - 700157, we cater to interior designers, architects, carpenters, building contractors, and retail buyers needing premium timber materials at competitive wholesale prices.

We proudly supply authentic products from India's most trusted manufacturers including Greenply, CenturyPly, MOD Ply & Board, Aikon Ply, and Pidilite Fevicol.
